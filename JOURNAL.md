# Line-Follower Robot — Project Journal

## Day 1 — Researching designs
I explored different line-follower robot designs and compared their general layouts to understand how the chassis, sensors, controller, and motors could fit together.

**Progress:** Initial design research completed.

## Day 2 — Finalising the concept
I finalised the line-follower robot as my project and learned the basic working principle: IR sensors detect the line, and the controller uses their readings to adjust the motors and steer the robot.

**Progress:** Project concept selected and working principle studied.

## Day 3 — Starting the chassis model
I created an Onshape account and started building the chassis model. I also considered where the main components should be placed on the chassis.

**Progress:** Onshape setup completed; chassis modelling and component placement started.

## Day 4 — Building the components
I continued building/modeling the components needed for the robot and developing the mechanical layout around the chassis.

**Progress:** Component modelling/building started.
